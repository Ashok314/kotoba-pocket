Small, by design.

For reading now. And remembering later.

⌘

Save your way.

Use the right-click menu, a keyboard shortcut, or the extension’s save button. Choose whatever fits your reading rhythm.

↗

Keep the source, too.

Save the word with its page title, URL, and timestamp. Return to the original page whenever you like. The same word won’t be saved twice.

↓

Yours to keep. Yours to export.

Browse your collection and remove words you no longer need. Export everything as a Japanese-friendly CSV file.

Three simple steps

Catch the word while it’s there.

  1. 01

    Select a word

    Highlight a word or short phrase on a regular web page as you read.

  2. 02

    Put it in your pocket

    Choose “Save to Kotoba Pocket” from the right-click menu, use the save button, or press your shortcut.

  3. 03

    Come back to it

    Open the extension to browse your words, revisit source pages, or download a CSV.

Just a shortcut away.

Save your selection without leaving the keyboard. Change the keys in Chrome’s extension shortcut settings.

If another extension uses the same keys, check the assigned shortcut in the popup.
Mac ⌘ + ⇧ + YWindows / Linux Ctrl + Shift + Y

Your words stay with you.

No login.
No cloud uploads.

Your saved vocabulary and source information stay in Chrome on this device. The extension has no ads or analytics and does not send your words to an external server.

Uninstalling the extension removes local entries. Export important vocabulary as CSV to keep a copy.
