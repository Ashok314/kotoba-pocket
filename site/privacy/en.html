Kotoba Pocket privacy

Kotoba Pocket stores words and phrases you select, the source page title and URL, and the time saved in Chrome's extension-local storage on this device.

Storage and use

The data is used to display your vocabulary, open source pages when requested, and create CSV downloads. It survives browser restarts. Uninstalling the extension removes local entries. You can delete individual entries. CSV export leaves them unchanged.

Data transmission

The extension does not upload vocabulary or page information to the developer, Google Drive or other servers. There are no analytics, advertising, accounts, tracking or remote executable code. Opening a source link navigates to that website at your request. CSV files are downloaded through the browser.

Page access

The popup reads the current selection for a preview. A word is saved only when you use the save button, context menu or keyboard shortcut. Password fields and browsing history are not read.

  • Storage permission keeps vocabulary and language preferences locally.
  • Temporary active-tab and scripting permissions retrieve selected text and display brief feedback after an extension action.
  • Context-menu permission provides the right-click save action.

Google Drive and Google Sheets integration are not included in this version.
