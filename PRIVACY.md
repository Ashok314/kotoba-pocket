# Privacy Policy — Kotoba Pocket

Kotoba Pocket saves vocabulary that you choose on web pages. Saved entries contain selected text, the source page title and URL, and the time saved.

## Storage and use

Data and your display-language preference are stored on the current device in Chrome's extension-local storage. It is used to display your vocabulary list, reopen source pages, and generate CSV files when you request an export. Browser restarts do not remove saved entries. Uninstalling the extension removes its local data.

The extension does not upload vocabulary or page information to the developer, Google Drive, or another server. It has no analytics, advertising, account system, tracking, or remote executable code. Selecting a source link opens that website as requested by you. CSV downloads are saved through your browser; the extension does not send them to another service.

The popup reads the selected text when opened to show a preview. The extension saves a record only after you use a save button, context-menu action or keyboard shortcut. It does not read password fields or scan your browsing history. Deletion removes the chosen entry from local extension storage. Exporting does not delete entries.

## Permissions

- `storage`: persist saved vocabulary on the current device.
- `activeTab`: temporarily access the page after an explicit extension action, context-menu action or keyboard shortcut.
- `scripting`: retrieve the selected text and show a brief save result on that page.
- `contextMenus`: provide the right-click save action for selected text.

The extension does not request permanent access to every website, browsing history, a Google account or Google Drive. Future Google Sheets integration is not included in this version.
